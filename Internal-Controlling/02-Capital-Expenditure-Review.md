## Investment request

Plant North proposes an automated inspection cell to reduce manual inspection and rework. Total investment is EUR 420,000.00: equipment EUR 360,000.00, installation EUR 45,000.00, and training EUR 15,000.00.

| Annual steady-state benefit | Amount EUR |
|---|---:|
| Reduced external inspection services | 90,000.00 |
| Reduced scrap and rework | 60,000.00 |
| Gross operating savings | 150,000.00 |
| Incremental maintenance cost | -18,000.00 |
| **Net operating benefit** | **132,000.00** |

Simple payback is EUR 420,000.00 / EUR 132,000.00 = approximately 3.18 years, excluding financing, tax, discounting, and ramp-up.

## Sensitivity

If gross savings are 20% lower, annual net benefit becomes EUR 102,000.00 and simple payback increases to approximately 4.12 years. Commissioning is planned for 2027-03-01; first-year benefits will be below the annual steady-state figure.

## Review conditions

Validate the external-service baseline, maintenance quotation, and acceptance criteria before approval. Treasury is separately reviewing a EUR 300,000.00 loan under DEMO-CR-2026-023; this review does not approve that financing.
