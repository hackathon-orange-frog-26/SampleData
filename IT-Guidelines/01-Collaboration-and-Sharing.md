## Purpose

Use approved collaboration workspaces to keep enterprise documents discoverable by the right people without exposing restricted information.

## Information handling

| Example classification | Fictional handling rule |
|---|---|
| Public | Only approved publication copies may be shared openly |
| Internal | Share with employees who need the information |
| Confidential | Use a restricted business workspace; external access needs owner approval |
| Highly Confidential | Limit access to explicitly authorized roles; external sharing requires an approved exception |

These are policy examples. Actual enforcement depends on configured permissions, sensitivity labels, and sharing controls.

## Required practices

- Store working copies in the appropriate Teams-connected or standalone SharePoint library.
- Use named-recipient links for sensitive business documents; verify recipients before sending.
- Keep salary records in the restricted Payroll workspace, not a general Finance channel.
- Review guest access at project closure and remove permissions that are no longer required.
- Report unintended sharing to the service desk immediately; do not assume deleting a chat message revokes file access.

## Example

A supplier may receive an approved purchase order but not the internal margin analysis, customer credit review, or employee payroll register stored elsewhere.

Document owners review access quarterly. The Digital Workplace team reviews this fictional guideline annually.
