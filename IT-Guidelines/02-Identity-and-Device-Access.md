## Employee access

Employees use individual enterprise identities and approved multifactor authentication. Shared personal accounts are not permitted. Access requests must identify a business purpose, an approving owner, and an expiration date when access is temporary.

Company-managed devices must meet the approved compliance baseline before accessing restricted workspaces. Users must report a lost device promptly and must not copy restricted documents to unmanaged storage to bypass an access issue.

## Privileged access

- Use a separate administrative identity for privileged tasks.
- Request time-limited elevation through the approved access process.
- Record the change or incident reference associated with administrative work.
- Review privileged role assignments monthly.
- Keep passwords, recovery codes, tokens, and private keys out of documents, chats, and repositories.

## Joiner, mover, and leaver controls

HR initiates onboarding and departure notifications. Managers approve business roles. Workspace owners review access when an employee changes department. IT Operations disables access at the agreed departure time and records completion.

## Exceptions and support

An exception requires documented risk acceptance, compensating controls, an accountable owner, and an expiry date. An exception request is not approval.

Use the approved internal service-desk channel for access problems. Never send authentication secrets to a support agent or an AI assistant.
